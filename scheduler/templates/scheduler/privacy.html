RiskFlow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

RiskFlow is a schedule and cost risk analysis tool. This policy explains what happens to your data — in short: it never leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing, on any server we control. RiskFlow has no accounts, no sign-in, no backend database, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. There is no server that RiskFlow talks to after the app itself loads.

Where your data lives

Every activity, estimate, and simulation setting you enter is stored only in your device's local app storage. It stays on your phone unless you explicitly export it yourself (Project → Export) as a JSON file that you choose where to save or share. Importing a file works the same way, entirely on-device.

Third-party services

The app loads one typeface ("IBM Plex Mono") from Google Fonts over the network so numeric columns render correctly. That request is subject to Google's own privacy policy. No other third-party service is used, and RiskFlow does not send any of your project data to Google or anyone else.

Children's privacy

RiskFlow is a professional project-scheduling tool, not directed at children, and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date above.
